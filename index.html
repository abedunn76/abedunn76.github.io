About

Abe Dunn is Assistant Chief Economist at the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) and a Research Fellow in the Department of Economics at the University of Vienna. His research spans health economics, industrial organization, and economic measurement.

Much of his work asks how to measure medical care correctly: its prices, its spending by disease, and its productivity. A second line of work uses new sources, such as card transactions and payroll records, to make official statistics faster and more geographically detailed. At BEA he oversees the Health Care Satellite Account and the agency’s near-real-time card spending statistics.

His research has appeared in the Quarterly Journal of Economics, Econometrica, American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics, the Journal of Health Economics, and Health Affairs. He is an editor of Forum for Health Economics & Policy and holds a Ph.D. from the University of Texas at Austin.
